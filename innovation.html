Redefining campus accessibility.

01
Core Project Value

Value at every level.

AccessReady delivers measurable value to students, universities and the broader campus community.

For Students

02
Three Key Innovations

What makes AccessReady unique.

Three interconnected innovations that create a complete accessibility ecosystem.

Process Innovation

A complete traceable end‑to‑end maintenance‑ticket workflow covering submission, verification, assignment, follow‑up and formal closure.

Impact: Clear responsible parties at every stage, connecting student user reports and official university‑maintenance procedures.

Collaborative Operating Model

Students identify and report on‑site physical barriers, while authorised university staff verify information, coordinate repairs and update public facility data.

Impact: AccessReady formalises student participation within official campus‑service processes — a true community collaboration.

Data Feedback Mechanism

Structured storage of barrier location, category, verification results and resolution status. Analyse repeated pain‑points to support accessibility audits and planning.

Impact: Universities gain evidence‑based insights for infrastructure upgrade decisions and long‑term accessibility strategy.

03
Innovation Framework

Designed through the SCAMPER method.

AccessReady was conceived using systematic creative thinking across seven dimensions.

S

Substitute

Verified dynamic accessibility information instead of static campus maps. Accessibility‑priority routing instead of shortest‑path navigation.

C

Combine

Accessible routes, facility status, user reports and university response combined into one integrated service and closed‑loop workflow.

A

Adapt

Crowdsourced incident reporting adapted to campus accessibility barriers. Maintenance‑ticket systems adapted to accessibility issues.

M

Modify

Route recommendations adjusted by mobility profiles and barrier severity. Facility data enhanced with verification timestamps and availability status.

P

Put to Another Use

Accessibility reports reused for maintenance prioritisation. Historical data reused for accessibility audits and campus planning.

E

Eliminate

Eliminate separate searches, phone calls and route trial‑and‑error. Eliminate duplicate reports and fragmented departmental handling.

R

Reverse

University‑issued alerts for known closures instead of waiting for individual complaints. Campus services adapt to users, not the other way around.

04
Our Edge

Three compelling differentiators.

Differentiator 01

More confident, independent journeys

Unlike general‑purpose map platforms, AccessReady focuses on mobility needs within university campuses. We combine mobility‑tailored routes with verified facility statuses and temporary‑barrier updates, helping students assess route suitability before travelling.

Differentiator 02

Problems that get follow‑up, not just reports

AccessNow provides crowdsourced accessibility information about places worldwide. AccessReady connects student reports with university verification, maintenance follow‑up and status updates — helping students understand whether reported barriers are being addressed.

Differentiator 03

Evidence‑based campus improvements

MazeMap already provides campus navigation, accessible routes and facilities‑management integration. AccessReady emphasises structured records of verified accessibility issues and their resolution — identifying recurring barriers, prioritising maintenance and informing long‑term planning.
