Project Overview

Bridging campus accessibility through digital intelligence.

AccessReady is a digital‑first platform designed for university campuses. It delivers personalised accessibility navigation, crowdsourced barrier reporting and closed‑loop university maintenance workflows — all in one unified system.

We bridge the gap between campus facility information, student needs and institutional improvement actions to support safe, independent campus movement for mobility‑limited users.

02
Platform Capabilities

Everything you need for accessible campuses

Three integrated modules working together to create a complete accessibility ecosystem.

Personalised Navigation

Mobility‑adjusted route planning tailored to each user's profile — wheelchair, crutches, or temporary injury.

  • Multiple mobility profile options
  • Real‑time facility status lookup
  • Ramp, elevator & restroom availability
  • Pre‑trip route assessment

Crowdsourced Reporting

Students report on‑site barriers with geotags, photos and descriptions — saved as drafts when offline.

  • Geotagged incident reporting
  • Photo attachment support
  • Offline draft saving
  • Public progress tracking

Maintenance Ticketing

Full‑cycle university workflow — verify, assign, track and close maintenance tickets with full traceability.

  • End‑to‑end ticket workflow
  • Multi‑level priority assignment
  • Proactive alert notifications
  • Audit‑ready data export

The Problem

Why existing solutions fall short.

Physical hardware alone cannot guarantee continuous, safe and independent movement across campus.

Static maps, dynamic hazards

Construction zones, blocked walkways and elevator breakdowns aren't captured on any static campus map.

Broken feedback loops

Students report issues but never see what happens next — no verification, no timeline, no closure.

Fragmented routes

Accessibility facilities suffer from "built but hard to use" — hardware exists on paper, not in daily reality.

Innovation & Differentiation

What sets AccessReady apart

Beyond navigation — a complete campus accessibility ecosystem.

Process Innovation

Complete traceable end‑to‑end maintenance‑ticket workflow covering submission, verification, assignment, follow‑up and formal closure.
