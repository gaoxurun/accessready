Target Audience

Designed for real people.

AccessReady serves two primary audiences and three important secondary groups across the university ecosystem.

01
Primary Audience

Who we serve first.

Two core user groups at the heart of everything we build.

End‑Users

Mobility‑Impaired Students

Permanently or temporarily mobility‑impaired university students including wheelchair users, crutch users and students recovering from injury.

Group Characteristics
  • Full‑time undergraduate and postgraduate students
  • Participate fully in all campus activities
  • Face high travel uncertainty daily
  • Strong willingness for independent living
Core Needs
  • Reliable pre‑trip route assessment
  • Real‑time facility status updates
  • Simple barrier‑reporting function
  • Visible feedback for submitted issues
Paying Customers

University Departments

University facility‑management departments and student‑service departments with statutory obligations for campus accessibility management.

Group Characteristics
  • Internal administrative departments
  • Scattered complaint channels
  • Accountable for regulatory compliance
  • Require traceable records
Core Needs
  • Unified workflow management
  • Compliance audit evidence
  • Data‑driven efficiency
  • Full traceability records

02
Secondary Audience

Extending impact across campus.

Three additional groups who benefit from AccessReady's accessibility ecosystem.

Maintenance Staff

Front‑line field workers who carry out physical repair and barrier clearance work across campus.

  • Clear location descriptions & photos
  • Accurate priority marking
  • Work order assignment on‑the‑go

Accessibility Working Groups

Policy‑planning, research‑oriented campus working groups focused on long‑term inclusive reform.

  • Aggregated barrier statistics
  • Renovation proposal evidence
  • Internal policy recommendations

Campus Visitors

External campus guests including visiting scholars, injured visitors and elderly family members of students.

  • Campus navigation for unfamiliar guests
  • Accessible route support
  • Facility availability info

Most students avoid frequently asking classmates or volunteers for assistance. They wish to reduce dependence on other people and control their own campus schedules.

03
User Journey

A day in the life.

How AccessReady transforms the typical campus experience for a mobility‑impaired student.

Morning — Plan the Day

Before leaving the dorm, the student opens AccessReady and selects their mobility profile. They check real‑time status of elevators and ramps along their route to the first class, confident the path is passable.

Encounter a Barrier

On the way to the library, they discover a construction barrier blocking the accessible ramp. Instead of being stranded, they quickly submit a geotagged report with a photo — saved as draft if signal is weak.

University Response

Facilities staff verify the report, create a maintenance ticket and assign high priority. The student receives a notification that their report has been received and work is scheduled.

Resolution & Closure

Repairs are completed within 48 hours. The platform updates the map and facility status. The student gets a notification that the barrier is resolved — the loop is closed, and the whole campus benefits.
